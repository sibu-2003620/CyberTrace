CYBER AWARENESS

Think before you click.

Simple habits that reduce common phishing risks.

🔎

Check the domain

Look carefully for misspellings, unexpected subdomains and unfamiliar domains.

🔒

HTTPS is not enough

A padlock indicates encrypted transport, not that the website itself is trustworthy.

📧

Question urgency

Be cautious when messages pressure you to verify, pay or act immediately.

🔑

Protect passwords

Use unique passwords and never disclose one-time codes to strangers.

📱

Verify messages

Use an organization's official website or app instead of a link from an unexpected message.

🚨

Report suspicious activity

If a message seems fraudulent, preserve evidence and report it through the relevant official channel.

Remember: CyberTrace is an educational risk indicator, not a guarantee that a URL is safe or malicious.
